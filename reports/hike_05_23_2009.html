Utah Canyoneering May 23-31, 2009

Introduction

Kathy: We had made plans to do a bunch of canyons off of Hole in the Rock (HITR) Road between Escalante, Utah and Lake Powell. We had been hearing how dry the area was and were trying to decide how to deal with things like the dry keeper pothole in Neon Canyon. However, a strange sort of monsoon weather pattern began as we drove to Utah! We ended up not doing any canyons off of HITR because the road becomes impassable when wet!

We are calling Dave McClintic "Wilbur" (from his famous performance in a 1st grade play) and Dave Cameron "Dave."

Saturday, May 23: Drive to Utah

Rogil: We changed plans in flight to UT. Ended up at Mosquito Cove south of Zion.

Kathy: Amazingly, we were able to get permits to do canyons in the park on the Memorial Day weekend. Yippee!

Sunday, May 24: Pine Creek and Behunin

Rogil: Dave, Rogil, Sheri, Julie and Bill went to Behounin. Counting the bus rides, it took 12 hours. Dave brought a 8mm rope and several of us rapped on it as a single line. No one seemed to have any problem. Notes: Rap 6 (some may remember as the 2 pitch rap with us high-lining people on the 2nd rap over a pool): this is a dirty nasty rap to get on if you go to the left. We went to the right and found 2 bolts and rapped to a large pothole which could easily be walked around and then continue rapping into a pool. Much simpler and prettier rap this way. Rap 8: I was last down. I stopped at the point where the ropes always stick, pulled the rope and set a new rap from this point. Very glad I did too as the rope didn't stick where I couldn't free it as in the past. The Pine folk got us campsites in Zion. Thanks again, that was so much better.

Kathy: Wilbur, Kim, Tom, Mark, Kathy and Tim went to Pine Creek. Mark and Kathy had done this canyon before but found much of it completely different than they remembered. This was the beginning of a pattern for Mark: All the canyons he repeated were like brand new canyons for him. Pine Creek had a lot more water--and colder water--the previous time. We got three vacated campsites at the South Campground in the park, so we only had to spend one night in the Mosquito Cove zoo.

Monday, May 25: Spry Canyon and Birch Hollow

Rogil: Dave, Rogil, Sheri, Julie, Kathy and Tim went to Birch Hollow (no permit needed if exiting upstream on Orderville). Steep approach, but not as bad as Mystery. We didn't count the raps, but everyone seemed to think it was around 12 (max length = 100'), plus 2 down-climbs that we protected. Several were 100' raps into cathedrals. Hike out was long and steep (1.5 hours, 1/2 uphill). 3 mile shuttle. A longer day (but no uphill) can be had by hiking down Orderville to the Narrows and take the bus out (6 hr hike out vs 1.5 hour hike out). Counting the shuttle, it was a 8 hour day.

Tuesday, May 26: Mystery Canyon and Hike Echo Canyon

Rogil: Sheri stayed in camp as she was ill. Dave, Rogil, Sheri, Julie and Kathy went on a hike up Echo Canyon. On the way back we swam up the narrows at the bottom of the Echo Canyon. Glad we didn't do Echo; I'm sure we would have all been hypothermic. Only spent 5 hours on the hike/swim. Headed on to Escalante. Stayed at Prospector Inn. Decent prices. Note: Hwy 12 has been renumbered and MP are off by about 1 mile.

Wednesday, May 27: Calf Creek

Rogil: Everyone went to Calf Creek. Took about 9 hours. Guide-books were mistaken about rap lengths: upper fall = 100', lower fall = 200'. Lower should be done using the bolts on the shelf as directionals next time or rap in the falls. The traverse was dangerous to the rope as 2 people fell sideways and the rope slid along the edge. Lower fall was at about the 1/2 way point in the canyon. The hike in the upper portion was unbelievably beautiful and fun. Camped at MP-70 on Hwy 12 on the east side just South of a cattle grate. This is Sheffield road. We camped in the wash which was roomy and flat, but we discovered a prettier spot 1.6 miles in: big vista and reasonably large site for next time.

Kathy: Any hiker can get to the top and bottom of Upper Calf Creek Falls, so the rappel there was optional. We saw other people back country hiking both up and down from the upper falls, and we saw some great places to camp for backpacking. A popular trail exists between the Calf Creek Campground and Lower Calf Creek Falls (which we hiked on the first Escalante trip in 1997). We car camped at Little Spencer Flat. Sheri found out about this great camping place from the Calf Creek Campground host.

Thursday, May 28: Middle Boulder Canyon

Rogil: Sheri went home; Julie moved in with Bill. We all went down Middle Boulder Canyon (7.5 hours) technical version. Tom and Mark headed up canyon at the confluence to head home, the rest went down stream. Seems some of our shoes and socks left at the cars collected very large rocks while we were away. Corrections: Hike starts at MP-76; hike SE to canyon; 5' drop from pot-hole was more like 10' drop; return hike is 2 miles on beautiful slick rock. Pot hole is hard, do not attempt with less than 3 people. If water is lower, could be a serious issue. Camped at the same place again.

Kathy: We misunderstood the directions at the start of this trip. The described "short distance" before dropping into the canyon probably should have been much shorter than what we did. This trip had nice variety.

Friday, May 29: Spencer Canyon

Rogil: Bill, Wilbur, and Kim left us. Moved Julie into our car and Kathy took her gear and Bill took our extra gear home for us (thank you). Went down Spencer Canyon, 8.5 hours. Only one 50' rap. We did 2 down-climbs that we protected and Tim down-climbed. The rap was the engineering of Tim and Dave. Using one of the spheres that erode out of the sandstone (1.5"), they put a sling around it and slipped it into a crack. We girth-hitched another sling to that one to reduce the chance of the sling moving relative to the ball, which it was wont to do. My fear was that the ball would be explosive at high pressure, so we backed it up for the first 4 people and Tim rapped just on the ball. There are 3 pools beyond the rap: 2 can be stemmed and one is a swimmer. At confluence, we went up the 3rd canyon on the right. It didn't appear to get much traffic. Next time go up the 2nd canyon. There is an escape from it on the left just before a large up-climb. Stayed at Prospector Inn again.

Kathy: The rap was into one of the darkest, narrowest bits of canyon we did this year. Tim has thoroughly proven that he is still rope gun material! And I'm still a wimpy weenie!

Tim: I recall going up the 2nd canyon on the right. The Kelsey description mentioned 200 meters from the confluence to the exit canyon? Wasn't that far. We had footprints leading in/out the canyon we went up. It didn't get narrow like Kelsey described. It was shorter that the canyon just prior.

Saturday, May 30: Hiked Bryce Canyon Rim Trail

Rogil: Bryce Rim trail. 5.5 mile trail, but we bailed after 3 miles due to lightning. Dave parked the shuttle car next to Kim and Wilbur's car. When we got back, Kim had left cookies for us (thanks!). Camped at White House trailhead. Checked on the Wave permits. Seems you have to enter the lottery for permits 24 hours in advance. Oh well, next time maybe.

Kathy: We camped with 85 boy scouts, and Julie did a great boy scout impression outside our tents--fooled us all!

Sunday, May 31: Hiked at Badger and Soap Washes

Rogil: Went down Badger and part-way down Soap before taking Julie to Flag and heading home. Kathy's brother Steve joined us for dinner at Touch of Thai when we got there.

Conclusion and General Notes

Rogil: Another great trip. Lots of new canyons and several worth repeating.

Kathy: Yes, this trip turned out great! What a great, fun group of people!
