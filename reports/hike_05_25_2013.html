Utah Canyoneering Trip May 25-June 2, 2013

Dave Cameron, Bill Fallon, Phil Goebel, Tom Kirsch, Tim Lange, Mark Lucas, Troy Lucas, Dave McClintic, Kim McClintic, Rogil Schroeter, Kathy Sharp, and Bernhard Stahuber went on the annual pilgrimage to visit Utah canyons this year.

We returned to the Robbers Roost area again, and it was even drier than last year . We had remarkably good temperatures for the whole trip this year. We altered our plans a bit and ended up with some shorter days due to possible rain, but the canyons we did were drier than last year. It was a great trip, even with no long, hot slogs or long, hard canyons.

Here's our trip report, along with links to Kathy's trip photos.

Day 1, Saturday May 25, 2013, Drive to Utah

We drove from Phoenix to the Robbers Roost area in Utah and camped at one of our favorite campsites in that area. My last Phoenix photo was at 8:17 a.m. and my first campsite photo was at 7:12 p.m. (I normally don't switch my camera to Utah time.)

Day 2, Sunday, May 26, 2013, Big Spring Canyon

We had intended to do the Big Spring Canyon East and West Fork loop, a new one for all of us. However, the first fork had a section that was harder than we expected based on the beta, and we thought we might have made a navigation mistake. We found the triple arch (and took lots of photos there), so we had not made a mistake. But when the beginning for the second fork wasn't looking as expected either, we decided to call it a day. My first hiking photo was at 9:38 a.m. and we were back at the cars at 2:37 p.m.

Day 3, Monday, May 27, 2013, High Spur

For our second day of canyons, we decided to do a canyon that we did last year and really enjoyed. High Spur was just as beautiful this year. A good number of my best photos this year were in this canyon. We gathered for the pre-hike photo at 7:16 a.m. and were back in camp by 2:28 p.m.

Day 4, Tuesday, May 28, 2013, Miss Piggy

We were looking to do some new canyons this year and decided to try the easiest of the Hog canyons. We had a fierce wind at the top of the first rappel, and managed to get a rope stuck for a while, but enjoyed the canyon. We started hiking at 10:59 a.m. and were back at the cars around 4:21 p.m.

Day 5, Wednesday, May 29, 2013, Hog One (aka Boss Hawg)

This canyon had been on our list for many years, but the description had always deterred too many of our group. The canyon requires lots of stemming with packs on bunny straps, and I don't do too well when I'm not on rope and have lots of open air below me. However, this year we had four who wanted to give it a shot, and since that's the max number of people who can comfortably (sort of) fit near one of the rappel stations, that worked well. The rest of our group went to play in Irish canyons. The Boss Hawg group had a pre-hike photo at 8:04 a.m. and was back at the cars at 1:51 p.m.

Day 6, Thursday, May 30, 2013, Mindbender

Six of us set off to do Mindbender, but Tom's knees were suffering, so he decided to head home early. (Kim and Dave also headed home this day.) The other five of us thoroughly enjoyed our time in the canyon, and went to hang out for quite a while at the exit for Not Mindbender, and then headed out via the Moqui Exit, which I'm thankful that Phil lead. (The horseflies were vicious on bare legs on the way to the exit, and they didn't let up until we started up the rock at the Moqui Exit.) We started hiking at 6:39 a.m. and were at Bill's truck at 4:34 p.m.

Day 7, Friday, May 31, 2013, Angel Cove Canyon

This was another new mini-slot for us, and Bill pointed out that it could be a great, easy introduction to many canyoneering techniques. Maybe that's why it appeared to have gotten a lot of use by guided groups, with roped hand lines already in place. It even had chain anchors around boulders. It looked like the guided groups probably didn't do the last two rappels, though. We started hiking at 9:10 a.m. and were back at the cars at 1:25 p.m.

Day 8, Saturday, June 1, 2013, Blarney Left and Right Forks

Mark, Troy, and Bernhard headed home in the morning, and the rest of us did the Left Fork of Blarney. Then we said goodbye to Bill and Phil, and the remaining four of us did the Right Fork of Blarney. We started hiking at 6:23 a.m., got back at 8:56 a.m., started again at 9:24 a.m., and got back to the cars at 12:50 p.m. Then we started our journey home, stopping at Muley Point to camp with a magnificent view.

Day 9, Sunday, June 2, 2013, Drive to Phoenix

We left Muley Point, had our traditional yummy breakfast at the restaurant at the bridge in Mexican Hat, stopped at the Cameron Trading Post for snacks and garden viewing, and then drove home, ending another great, fun Utah canyoneering trip with good friends.
