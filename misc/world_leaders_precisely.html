How Political Scientists Evaluate Leaders

Actual Government Actions vs. Political Attacks

Political experts don't judge world leaders by what their opponents call them on TV or social media. Instead, they look at three practical tests:

  • Respect for Rules: Does the leader protect independent courts, free elections, and a free press, or try to control them?
  • Business Ownership: Does the government own all companies (Socialism/Communism) or let people own businesses while regulating them (Capitalism)?
  • Campaign Style: Does the leader focus on national laws and equal rules (Liberalism/Conservatism) or pit "everyday citizens" against "corrupt insiders" (Populism)?

Constitutional Democracy

Joe Biden

President of the United States (2021–2025) Classification: Modern Social Liberalism / Regulated Capitalism
Accurate Political Description: “Biden belongs to the modern social liberal tradition—using federal spending and taxes to support workers and clean energy while protecting private business ownership.”
“Modern American liberalism, represented by mainstream Democratic leaders, combines a commitment to constitutional democracy and individual rights with federal regulatory intervention to support middle-class social safety nets.” — Quoted from Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Liberalism ↗
Common Social Media Misclassification: “Biden’s federal spending bills and student loan forgiveness plans are pure government-run socialism.”
Academic Analysis & Main Concerns: “Economists describe Biden's approach as government-guided capitalism rather than socialism. The main concern scholars raise is that large federal spending bills increase national debt and cause trade tensions with allies by giving extra money to American factories.”
Better Test: Giving government funding to private companies is not socialism because private investors still own the companies and keep the profits. Biden is a modern liberal operating within capitalism.
Right-Wing Populism / Civic Nationalism

Donald Trump

45th & 47th President of the United States Classification: Right-Wing Populism / Strong-Executive Nationalism
Accurate Political Description: “Trump fits the political science definition of right-wing populism—rallying everyday voters against political elites while pushing for higher trade tariffs and strict border controls.”
“Populism is a thin-centered ideology that considers society to be ultimately separated into two homogeneous and antagonistic groups: 'the pure people' versus 'the corrupt elite.'” — Quoted from University of Notre Dame: Overview of Populism ↗
Common Social Media Misclassification: “Trump's demand for total presidential immunity proves he is turning America into an absolute monarchy or a fascist dictatorship.”
Academic Analysis & Main Concerns: “Constitutional scholars warn that pushing for full legal immunity and threatening political opponents stretches democratic rules to their limit. However, because power in America is still decided by elections, state laws, and federal courts, political scientists classify this as a strain on a republic rather than a monarchy or fascism.”
Better Test: Trump uses populist rhetoric and pushes for strong presidential power inside a constitutional republic, which is distinct from an absolute king or a military dictatorship.

Olaf Scholz

Chancellor of Germany Classification: Social Democracy / Social Market Economy
Accurate Political Description: “Scholz represents classic German social democracy—combining strong worker union benefits and public healthcare with a major corporate export economy.”
“The German 'Social Market' model integrates free-market competition with constitutional guarantees of worker representation and universal social safety nets.” — Quoted from University of Texas at Austin: Political Glossary ↗
Common Social Media Misclassification: “Germany’s high income taxes and green energy laws prove it has given up on capitalism for state socialism.”
Academic Analysis & Main Concerns: “European policy experts focus on Scholz's efforts to rebuild Germany's military while cutting off Russian gas. Analysts point to structural friction between Germany's strict legal limit on government borrowing and the large funds needed to upgrade factories and energy grids.”

Emmanuel Macron

President of France Classification: Centrist Liberalism / European Union Integration
Accurate Political Description: “Macron represents centrist European liberalism—supporting business tax cuts, flexible employment laws, and strong cooperation across the European Union.”
“Liberalism is committed to individual freedom, rule of law, and market enterprise, combining classical market efficiency with modern European constitutional guarantees.” — Quoted from Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Liberalism ↗
Common Social Media Misclassification: “Macron using special constitutional rules to raise the retirement age proves he is a dictator who ignores democracy.”
Academic Analysis & Main Concerns: “Constitutional scholars note that while French law allows the president to pass certain budget laws without a full vote, doing so often makes voters feel ignored. Experts analyze this as a sign of a divided parliament rather than an authoritarian dictatorship.”
Better Test: Using legal constitutional powers in a country with open elections and free courts is not dictatorship. Macron is a centrist pro-business liberal.
Voting without Full Checks

Viktor Orbán

Prime Minister of Hungary Classification: Electoral Authoritarianism / National Conservatism
Accurate Political Description: “Orbán created what he calls an 'illiberal democracy'—keeping public elections, but changing voting laws and buying up news outlets so his party rarely loses.”
“Authoritarian regimes in the modern era frequently preserve formal elections while systematically eroding competitive political playing fields, press freedom, and judicial independence.” — Quoted from University of Michigan Press: Authoritarian Systems ↗
Common Social Media Misclassification: “Orbán is just an ordinary conservative leader who cares about protecting his country's borders.”
Academic Analysis & Main Concerns: “Political scientists highlight Hungary as a warning example of how a democracy can erode from the inside. Experts point out that while people still vote, the government controls almost all newspaper and TV stations, making true political competition nearly impossible.”
Better Test: Traditional conservatism supports independent courts and fair rules. Weakening courts and news outlets to stay in power moves a government from democracy into electoral authoritarianism.
One-Man Autocracy

Vladimir Putin

President of Russia Classification: One-Man Dictatorship / Military Nationalism
Accurate Political Description: “Putin runs an authoritarian dictatorship—jailing political rivals, shutting down independent news stations, and ruling through a tight circle of loyal allies.”
“Authoritarian regimes are characterized by political centralization, severe restrictions on genuine political competition, and the systematic erosion of formal democratic institutions.” — Quoted from University of Michigan Press: Authoritarian Systems ↗
Common Social Media Misclassification: “Russia under Putin is still a Soviet communist government.”
Academic Analysis & Main Concerns: “Scholars emphasize that Putin ended the old Soviet economic system long ago. Today, Russia is run by a wealthy group of state-connected business allies and a wartime military economy, holding onto power through fear and aggressive nationalism.”
Better Test: Russia replaced communist government ownership with private corporate wealth controlled by Putin's close allies. It is a modern dictatorship, not a communist state.
Single-Party State

Xi Jinping

General Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party & President of China Classification: Single-Party Communist State / State-Controlled Capitalism
Accurate Political Description: “Xi leads a single-party Communist state that combines strict political control over its people with state-directed market businesses.”
“In Marxist-Leninist single-party systems, the political party exercises supreme oversight over state organs, military forces, and strategic economic sectors.” — Quoted from Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Karl Marx ↗
Common Social Media Misclassification: “China is completely capitalist today because it allows private companies, tech billionaires, and stock markets.”
Academic Analysis & Main Concerns: “China experts note that Xi has centralized more personal power than any Chinese leader since Mao Zedong. Experts highlight his crackdown on private tech companies and real estate firms to ensure private business wealth never challenges the Communist Party's authority.”
Better Test: While China permits private businesses, the ruling Communist Party holds ultimate authority over every company and citizen, making it a single-party communist state.
