Annual Superstition Ridge Line Hike

Rogil and Bill usually organize this annual winter day hike along the entire ridge line of Superstition Mountain. Most of these notes are stolen (with permission) from Rogil's trip information. This trip is for the people Rogil and Bill invite.

One version of the hike starts from the Carney Springs trailhead , goes along the ridge line, goes down Siphon Draw, and ends at Lost Dutchman State Park ( LDSP map ).

The other version of the hike goes the opposite direction, starting from the Siphon Draw trailhead and ending at the Carney Springs trailhead.

Everyone goes the same direction, but for logistical reasons, we divide the group into two. Rogil organizes a slightly earlier group of hikers and Bill organizes the later hikers.

If you plan to go on the hike, let the organizer of your group (early or late) know well before the trip. The organizers need to know this to organize the car shuttle, etc. Do not just show up unless you are doing your own shuttle. Also let the organizers know whether you are driving and how many passengers and packs you can carry. We always need volunteers to shuttle!

Trail

The trail is 13 miles. It usually takes around 9 hours. The first 1.5 hours are steep uphill (gaining over 2000 feet in 2 miles, maybe), the last 2 hours are steep downhill, and the in-between part is much easier. But this is still on the order of a Grand Canyon hike. The views are spectacular! The mountain itself is Rogil's favorite part of the scenery with hoodoos and rock outcroppings.

Do not get lost! Stay with someone who for sure knows the route.

If you finish the hike and leave before your organizer, please leave a note on your organizer's vehicle or cell phone so the organizer will know who made it off the mountain.

For the elevation profile, see below.

Schedule

Take note of the sunrise and sunset for the day of the hike to determine how much daylight you will have for the hike.

The earlier hikers meet Saturday morning in the parking lot south of Macaroni Grill on the southeast corner of US 60 and Stapley Drive in Mesa. We run the shuttle from there, dropping vehicles at the hiking-route end and regrouping at the hiking-route start. The meeting time for shuttle drivers is 5:10 a.m. Those not driving a shuttle meet at 5:40 a.m. Arrive at Macaroni Grill BEFORE the meeting time.

(Note that in 2012, the gate at LDSP didn't open till 6:00 a.m.)

The earlier group should be on the trail BEFORE 6:30 a.m.

The later group starts hiking at 7:00 a.m.

Bill's group meets at the hiking-route end at 6:00 a.m. and everyone leaves in the shuttle vehicles no later than 6:15 a.m. Bill's group is at the hiking-route start at 6:45 a.m. to begin hiking no later than 7:00 a.m.

Do not leave anything in the shuttle vehicle before the hike if it isn’t your vehicle. You will likely not see that vehicle again after the hike.

Bring

It will be cold at least in the morning. There's no shade on most of the trail. And NO water. So:

  • Wear layered clothing, a hat and wind breaker. Be prepared for any weather.
  • Bring sun protection.
  • Bring a full day's supply of water (minimum of 3 quarts or liters). Rogil will probably carry 5 quarts.
  • Rogil recommends a walking stick, a flashlight and FRS radios (channel 7 code 0; to connect the group when we get spread out).
  • And of course, bring lunch and maps.

Don't forget to carry your car keys with you while hiking.

Also, bring exact change for the entrance fee to LDSP ($7?) in case the ranger office is closed.

Please Note

This is a strenuous hike for experienced, fit people only.

There will be NO sweep (unless someone wants the job), so if you find yourself falling too far behind, please consider returning to the start instead of finishing. Too far behind is arriving at the first summit after 10:00 a.m. Call your group organizer's cell phone if you turn around. Cell phones won't necessarily work on the mountain, but you can leave voice mail.

If you plan to carry your phone, please give your organizer your number. Better yet, reply-all to a trip email to give all the hikers your number.

Directions

Macaroni Grill

From US-60, get off at Stapley Drive. Go south. Macaroni Grill is on the left a little ways south. Park on the south side of Macaroni Grill a few rows away from the restaurant. If its lights are off (they usually are), look for Party Place just south of it.

Carney Springs

Take US-60 to the turn off for Peralta Trail (there is a traffic light there). Turn north (left). The Carney Springs trailhead is .6 mile past the sign saying 1.5 miles to Peralta trailhead (or 5.2 miles from the end of the paved road). A small parking lot will be on your left. Park cars to allow room for lots of vehicles.

Note: in getting to Carney from LDSP, you can take a shortcut south on Mt. View Rd. to the US-60. Mt. View Rd. is 2 miles southwest from the LDSP entrance. Go east (left turn) on US-60 (see directions above to Carney).

Lost Dutchman

Get off US-60 at Idaho Rd, go north to SR-88, go northeast (right turn) to Lost Dutchman SP, turn right into the park, and pay the entrance fee. We park at the Siphon Draw trailhead at the far end of the road in the picnic area.

From the east on US-60, turn north on Mt. View Rd. at the light just before the freeway begins. Go north to the Apache Trail (SR-88). Turn right. Go 2 miles to LDSP (see above).
