2014 Havasu Canyon 4-Day Trip

Photos from past trips

Kathy organizes an annual trip to Havasu Canyon for her friends. The trip is usually in mid-May. We plan to hike down on Friday, camp 3 nights in the canyon, and hike up on Monday. See more trip details and other links at Havasu Canyon Links. (Once organized and conditions permitting, the trip happens whether Kathy goes or not.)

I do not plan to organize or go on the Havasupai trip this year. If others want to simply provide a link they are willing to share with the public (for example, from MeetUp ) on this web page to organize their own trip, just let me know.

February 2, 2013

We have reservations for 30 people to hike in on Friday, May 17, 2013 and hike out on Monday, May 20, 2013. (Five people could only go on that weekend, while only two people were limited to the first weekend.)

I did not pay anything, so don't pay me anything until right before the trip.

Since we have so many people going and we thus have to make our reservation so early, we always end up with some people who eventually find that they cannot go on the trip. If you'd like to be on the waiting list to consider taking any openings, let me know.

It's time to start training for a hike to waterfall heaven!:o)

--Kathy

January 26, 2013

I am planning the annual Havasu Canyon trip for hiking into the canyon on Friday, May 10 or 17, 2013 and hiking out on Monday, May 13 or 20. I do not want to attract a huge number of people, but I do want to enjoy the canyon with all my family, friends, and co-workers who are up to this kind of trip.:o)

We normally drive up on a Thursday night and camp on the rim; hike down into Havasu Canyon on Friday; camp by beautiful, blue-green Havasu Creek in the Grand Canyon on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday nights; hike up and out on Monday; and arrive back in Phoenix Monday evening.

The tribe says they will start accepting reservations on February 1st this year and the prices will be the same as last year. Here are the prices I charged (including my overhead costs) for 2012:

  • The full cost per person for 3 nights at the campground: around $104.
  • Optional shared pack horses: $200 round trip.
  • Children: no discount any more.

Trip detail links are at my Havasu Canyon Links web page. Please check there before asking me questions. If you’d like to go on this trip, please email me before February that you’re interested, and I’ll continue the trip conversation with just the interested people.

I'm getting this message out late, so if I get through to the tribe next weekend, I may just reserve spots for 20 people and let people claim spots (by promising to reimburse me), first-come, first-served. I could start a waiting list and try changing the reservation later if my guess is way off.

Thanks, and I hope I’ll see you outdoors this year!

Kathy
